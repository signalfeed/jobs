# How to get business leads from Google Maps

Google Maps is the most complete directory of local businesses: dentists in Austin, roofers in Denver, cafes in Chicago. Here are two ways to turn it into a lead spreadsheet, one paid and one free.

## Option 1: Google Maps Scraper on Apify (most complete)

[Google Maps Scraper](https://apify.com/compass/crawler-google-places?fpr=kyk4x5) is the most-used tool on the Apify Store, with nearly 700,000 users. You give it a search ("dentist") and a place ("Austin, Texas"), and it returns every matching listing.

1. [Create a free Apify account](https://apify.com?fpr=kyk4x5). The free plan includes monthly credit, enough for a few hundred leads to test with.
2. Open [Google Maps Scraper](https://apify.com/compass/crawler-google-places?fpr=kyk4x5), enter your search terms and location, and set the maximum number of places.
3. Turn on contact details if you want emails and social profiles (it visits each business's website).
4. Click **Start**, then export the results as CSV or Excel.

**What it costs** (pay per result, November 2026): about **$4 per 1,000 places**, plus about **$2 per 1,000** when you add contact details. A 1,000-lead list with contacts runs roughly $6.

**What you get:** name, category, address, phone, website, rating, review count, opening hours and, with contact details on, emails and social links.

If you only need emails, [Google Maps Email Extractor](https://apify.com/lukaskrivka/google-maps-with-contact-details?fpr=kyk4x5) combines both steps in one run.

## Option 2: OpenStreetMap (free)

OpenStreetMap lists millions of businesses under an open license (ODbL), so the data is free to reuse with credit. Coverage is strong for healthcare, legal, real estate and restaurants and thinner for trades. The full Python method is here: [build a free local business lead list with OpenStreetMap](local-business-lead-list-openstreetmap.md).

## Add emails to any list of websites

Both options give you websites; most business emails live on the site's contact page. [Website Contact Extractor](https://apify.com/signalfeed/website-contact-extractor?fpr=kyk4x5) pulls public emails, phones and social profiles from a list of websites for $2.50 per 1,000 sites, or see [the code to do it yourself](extract-emails-from-website.md).

## Using leads responsibly

Stick to publicly listed business contact details, honor opt-outs, and follow CAN-SPAM, GDPR and your local rules when you reach out. Check Google's terms for how you plan to use the data.
